
The daemon restarts a supervised shunt when restart-only settings change in either direction, after `shunt check` passes with the gateway's cwd and environment. This includes server bind, concurrency, shutdown, access control, header/URL limits, rate limits, boot-only subsystem presence, spend/pool state paths and poll cadence, status polling, sentry and otel. Hot-reloadable edits and formatting changes keep the child. Invalid edits keep the running child and log a refusal once; unchanged refusals are rechecked after 60 seconds. An unreadable restart setting also logs once per config digest and keeps the child until repaired. If the spawn-time settings could not be read, the first readable settings require a successful check and respawn before becoming the baseline. Shutdown cancels an in-flight check; killed check children are reaped separately so stalled child I/O cannot hold the supervisor past its stop budget.
